# Business Recommendation on Advertising Investment

## Executive Summary

The Meridian Marketing Mix Model indicates that the company should prioritize
targeted budget reallocation rather than increasing spending equally across all
channels.

Google Paid Search is the strongest opportunity for additional investment. It
generated the highest historical ROI, the highest marginal ROI, and the best
profit result in the scenario simulations.

Meta Facebook and Meta Instagram remain important volume-driving channels, but
they should be scaled cautiously because they already receive most of the
historical media budget.

Google PMAX, Google Shopping, and Google Video should be reduced, held, or
reviewed because of saturation or weak marginal returns.

## Key Findings

### Channel Contribution

The largest estimated contributions to purchases were:

| Channel | Contribution |
|---|---:|
| Meta Instagram | 35.90% |
| Meta Facebook | 27.56% |
| Google Paid Search | 16.84% |
| Baseline demand | 17.66% |

Meta channels generated the largest purchase volume, while Google Paid Search
generated a strong contribution despite receiving a much smaller share of
historical media spend.

### ROI and Marginal ROI

| Channel | Historical ROI | Marginal ROI | Recommended Action |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Google Paid Search | 11.53 | 5.41 | Increase investment |
| Meta Facebook | 2.40 | 1.42 | Maintain or cautiously scale |
| Meta Instagram | 2.33 | 1.33 | Maintain or cautiously scale |
| Google Shopping | 1.39 | 0.46 | Hold or reduce |
| Google PMAX | 1.13 | 0.44 | Hold or reduce |
| Google Video | 0.89 | 0.40 | Review or reduce |

Google Paid Search is the strongest under-invested channel because its marginal
ROI is substantially above 1.

Google PMAX and Google Shopping show weaker marginal efficiency and stronger
signs of saturation. Google Video is below break-even based on both historical
ROI and marginal ROI.

## Investment Scenario Results

| Scenario | Revenue Change | Purchase Change | Estimated Profit Change | Additional-Spend ROI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Paid Search spend +50% | $980,398 | 11,608 | $760,543 | 4.46 |
| Total budget +20%, equal allocation | $2,271,142 | 27,018 | $453,296 | 1.25 |
| Random half of channels +10% | $815,244 | 9,861 | $306,115 | 1.60 |
| PMAX and Shopping spend -30% | -$66,816 | -819 | $59,320 | Cost-saving scenario |

The equal-allocation scenario generated the highest absolute revenue uplift,
but it required considerably more investment and produced weaker efficiency.

The Paid Search +50% scenario generated the highest estimated profit improvement
and the strongest return on additional spending.

## Recommended Next-Quarter Investment Strategy

The recommended strategy is:

1. Increase Google Paid Search investment first.
2. Maintain Meta Facebook and Meta Instagram as core acquisition channels.
3. Test additional Meta spending gradually because Meta already dominates the
   historical budget.
4. Reduce or hold Google PMAX and Google Shopping until their marginal returns
   improve.
5. Review Google Video and reduce spending unless it supports important
   brand-awareness objectives.
6. Avoid distributing new budget equally across all channels.
7. Re-estimate the MMM regularly as new marketing and revenue data becomes
   available.

## Final Assignment Questions

### 1. What is the recommended marketing investment strategy for the next quarter, and why?

The company should prioritize additional investment in Google Paid Search.

Paid Search achieved a historical ROI of 11.53 and a marginal ROI of 5.41. In
the scenario analysis, increasing Paid Search spending by 50% required
approximately $219,855 in additional budget and generated:

- $980,398 in additional revenue
- 11,608 additional purchases
- $760,543 in estimated additional profit
- 4.46 return on the additional spend

Meta Facebook and Meta Instagram should remain important channels, but further
growth should be controlled. PMAX and Shopping should be held or reduced due to
saturation and weak marginal returns. Video should be reviewed because its
returns are below break-even.

### 2. What is the best strategy toward revenue maximization?

For maximum absolute revenue uplift, the equal +20% total-budget scenario
produced the largest result, generating approximately $2.27 million in
additional revenue.

However, for profitable and efficient revenue growth, the Paid Search +50%
scenario was the best strategy. It produced the highest estimated profit
increase and the strongest return on additional investment.

Therefore:

- Maximum absolute revenue: increase the total budget by 20%
- Maximum profit and efficiency: increase Google Paid Search by 50%
- Recommended business strategy: prioritize Google Paid Search

## Limitations

- The model represents one selected eCommerce organization and time period.
- MMM results depend on observational data and causal assumptions.
- Some smaller channels may have greater posterior uncertainty.
- SHAP explains predictive patterns in a surrogate model, not causal
  incrementality.
- Scenario simulations are model-based estimates and do not guarantee future
  performance.
- Recommended budget changes should be introduced gradually and validated
  through controlled campaign experiments.
